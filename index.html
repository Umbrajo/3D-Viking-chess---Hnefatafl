Rules of this adaptation

The king starts at the centre of a 343-square cube. 24 defenders protect him; 54 attackers approach from the six faces.

Each piece slides along one of the X, Y, or Z axes without jumping. Only the king may occupy the centre and the twelve exits. Other pieces may pass through the empty centre.

Capture a piece by sandwiching it between the moved piece and an ally along any of the three axes. An empty exit or the empty centre may act as a supporting square. The king can take part in captures.

The king escapes by reaching one of the cube’s eight corners or one of the four corners on the central horizontal plane L4: a1, a7, g1, and g7. Exits are marked in gold. The starting position is symmetrical on all three axes: 24 defenders around the king and nine attackers at the centre of each of the six faces. To capture the king, attackers must close all six neighboring directions: an attacker, the empty centre, or a cube boundary closes a direction. The capture must be completed by a move adjacent to the king. A side with no legal moves loses; three repetitions draw the game.

These are original experimental rules whose balance is still being refined; they are not historical Hnefatafl rules.
